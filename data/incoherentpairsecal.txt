# notes: effective cross section sigma_eff = N(e+-, p_T > 1.4 GeV) / L_crossing, so that sigma_eff x L_collider is the true rate of pair leptons entering the detector. Beam parameters from arXiv:2407.12450 Table 1.1, Scenario 1 (Stage 1 at 3 TeV, Stage 2 at 10 TeV); see guineapig/ for the inputs and the runner. Averaged over 84 bunch crossings at 3 TeV and 112 bunch crossings at 10 TeV. Uncertainty is the crossing-to-crossing standard error only. This file holds the effective cross section; the figure plots it against the rate axis instead, rescaled by L(sqrt_s)/L_nominal so each stage reads its own true rate (see mcrates.rate_equivalent). p_T is taken at production: the beam-field deflection that pair tracking would add is not included (track_pairs=0, which segfaults in this build), and that omission matters far more at this threshold than at 15 MeV. The 3 TeV point rests on a single lepton above threshold, so treat it as an order of magnitude.
