Averaged over 24 bunch crossings at 3 TeV and 24 bunch crossings at 10 TeV. Uncertainty is the crossing-to-crossing standard error only. p_T is taken at production: the beam-field deflection that pair tracking would add is not included (track_pairs=0, which segfaults in this build), and that omission matters far more at this threshold than at 15 MeV.
