Expected file format

One row per point, comma- or whitespace-separated: centre-of-mass energy in TeV first, cross section second. Lines starting with # are comments. A # columns: line gives the units, exactly as the files in data/ do, and is picked up automatically. 

# title: VBF ZH
# process: mu+ mu- -> Z H nu nubar
# source: MadGraph5_aMC@NLO, leading order
# columns: sqrt_s [TeV], sigma [fb]
1.0, 12.4
3.0, 41.9
10.0, 96.3

Nothing you add here leaves your browser — it is kept in local storage only. Use Download as data file to get a repository-ready copy, then open a pull request.

Reading the figure

The left axis is the cross section in femtobarns. The right axis converts it to an interaction rate at the nominal 10 TeV operating point, L = 2 × 1035 cm−2 s−1, so the two axes are the same numbers in different clothes.

Curve families

  • Machine-induced & inclusive (grey) — what the detector sees whether or not anything interesting happened: inclusive hadron production, soft dijets, incoherent e+e− pairs from the beam-beam interaction, and neutrino interactions in the detector slice.
  • Standard Model — annihilation and vector-boson-fusion processes, the physics programme itself.
  • BSM benchmarks — thermal-relic WIMP pair production, where the mass is fixed by the relic abundance so the curve turns on at threshold.
  • Also in the dataset — series that ship in data/ but are off by default because they would crowd the reference figure.

Guide lines

The dashed horizontals mark the LHC bunch-crossing and Level-1 rates, the 30 kHz crossing rate of a 10 km muon collider ring, and one event per Snowmass year (107 s) — the practical floor of the physics reach.

Contribute a curve

Everything on this page comes from plain text files in the repository. Adding a process is a small pull request, and corrections are just as welcome as new data.

  1. Prototype it here: drop your file into Add your own curve and check that it lands where you expect.
  2. Hit Download as data file to get a copy with the standard header already filled in.
  3. Add it to data/, register it in mcrates.py, and open a pull request.
